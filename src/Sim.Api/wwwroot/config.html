Neighbourhood battery and peak shaving

With this off the neighbourhood has no storage at all, the control strategy never runs, and the dashboard shows a single net load line.

kWh of usable storage. It starts each run at half full.

Energy out divided by energy in over a full cycle, 10 to 100 %. Sent to the API as a fraction, so 90 % is stored as 0.90.
Optional hard ceiling, 0 = percentile strategy only. Above 0 the battery also discharges to hold the neighbourhood under this many kW, on top of its normal behaviour of discharging in the top 20 % of observed load and recharging in the bottom 40 %.
Saving restarts the simulation. The neighbourhood is rebuilt from the new seed, the ledger and both peak counters are reset to zero, and 24 simulated hours are replayed immediately so the chart is full on the first paint. Nothing from the previous run is carried over.
